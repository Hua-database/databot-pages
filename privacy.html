Databot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

Databot is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's Google authorization, it reads and writes only the owner's Google Sheets and creates spreadsheet copies in the owner's Google Drive, to store statistics.

What it stores

The Google authorization token is stored only on the owner's own computer and server. No other personal data is collected.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
